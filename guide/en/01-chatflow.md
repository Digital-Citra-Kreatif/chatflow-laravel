# Chat forms (ChatFlow)

ChatFlow forms ask their questions one at a time, like a chat. You find them under
**ChatFlow** in the sidebar: **Chat forms** to build them and **Chat submissions** to read the answers.

## Creating a form

1. Open **Chat forms** and click **New form**.
2. Start blank, pick a template, or import a form someone exported.
3. The builder opens. Your changes save themselves every few seconds; the badge next to
   the title says **Saved** when they have.

## Building the conversation

- **Questions**: click **Add field** to add a question after the one you selected, drag
  the handles to reorder, and edit the selected question on the right: what the bot says,
  whether an answer is required, the choices.
- **Endings**: what the visitor sees at the end, or a page they go to.
- **Rules** (on a question) and **Logic**: skip questions or choose the ending based on
  answers. A choice can also lead to a question of its own (option → **More**).
- **Design**: colours and style, with the chat beside it as visitors will see it.
- **Settings**: where the form shows, saving and resuming, privacy, spam protection and
  the chat's built-in texts.
- **Emails**: emails to your team and to the visitor after a submission.

Nothing reaches visitors until you click **Publish**. After that, edits stay in the draft
until you publish again; the badge says **Unpublished changes** meanwhile.

## Sharing

The **Share** tab has the form's link, a QR code and the code a developer pastes into the
website.

## Reading submissions

**Chat submissions** lists the answers, newest first. Unread ones have a dot. Use the tabs for
partial and abandoned conversations, and the filters to find someone. Open a submission to
read the answers or the whole conversation, add a private note, change its status or send
its emails again. **Export CSV** downloads one form's submissions for a spreadsheet.
